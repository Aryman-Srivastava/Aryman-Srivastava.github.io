        01 · About
        
I build systems that turn complexity into clarity.

      

      

        
At NatWest Group in Gurugram, India, I work as an SDE II on the orchestration layer for a multi-agent system built on top of our RAG pipeline, along with the data engineering (MongoDB), messaging (Kafka), and authentication (JWKS, IAM) that keep it reliable in production.

        
Much of that work centers on software design patterns that keep the orchestration layer extensible, and on tools like Agent SDK and Opik for building and monitoring agentic workflows. Beyond individual delivery, this has grown to include supporting — and at times leading — teammates through their own work on these projects.

        
That foundation traces back to IIIT Delhi, where a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (2024) followed research stints in generative AI and adversarial machine learning. The common thread throughout has been building things that measurably save someone time, not just demos.
